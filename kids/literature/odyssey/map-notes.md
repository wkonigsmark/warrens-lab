# Odyssey Map Notes

This project should keep the map flexible. The Odyssey moves through real Greek places, mythic islands, possible historical echoes, and deliberately uncertain geography. The first job is to track the sequence clearly; later versions can layer interpretive maps on top.

## Map Buckets

- Known place: a real-world place or region strongly connected with the story.
- Mythic place: a story location that should be treated primarily as legendary.
- Mythic/possible real place: a story location with famous proposed real-world identifications, but no single certainty.
- Spiritual place: a non-ordinary location, such as the underworld.
- Uncertain route: a travel segment where the story matters more than exact geography.

## Future Map Concepts

- Story path mode: simple sequence from node to node, prioritizing readability.
- Mythic sea mode: islands and monsters arranged as a legendary journey, not a claim of real geography.
- Historical theories mode: optional overlays showing proposed real-world identifications.
- Kid mode: fewer labels, more icons, simplified route.
- Parent mode: notes about ancient geography, scholarly uncertainty, and later traditions.
