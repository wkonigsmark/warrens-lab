# Wizarding Quiz — Roadmap

## v1 (shipped)
- Endless forgiving quiz stream: Book I, Book II, Grand Mix
- 5 mechanics: multiple choice · true/false · Who Said It? · Order the Events · Odd One Out
- 3 categories: 📖 Story · 💖 Heart & Courage · ⭐ Superfan (double points)
- House points + house picker theming, 🔥 streaks, chocolate frog card collection
- Books 3–7 locked on the shelf
- Mobile-first, starry-night visual theme

## Ideas / backlog
- **Book 3+** question banks as they're read (unlock the shelf one book at a time)
- **Sound** — gentle chime on correct, page-turn on next (with a mute toggle)
- **Two-player mode** — pass-and-play, Gryffindor vs. Slytherin scoreboard
- **Daily Prophet mode** — 10-question "edition of the day" with a printable score
- **More frog cards** + rare "golden" cards for superfan streaks
- **Read-aloud button** (speech synthesis) for pre-readers playing along
- **Picture rounds** — "which spell/scene is this?" once we have art
