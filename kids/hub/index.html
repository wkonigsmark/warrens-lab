Curriculum

Ballard Science

Beetle / Mealworm Unit

1st graders have been studying grass, alfalfa, and wheat seeds as part of the K-2 science animal and plant investigation unit. The class also observed mealworms transforming into darkling beetles. The beetle unit is now concluding.

Edie Other

Día de Español

Students are performing in a Spanish-language cultural performance event called Día de Español, reflecting Spanish language and cultural learning in the Red Room AM class.

Edie Other

Class Book Project: K-Pop Demon Hunters Y Su Aventura à la Playa

Students collaborated over several weeks to create a class book combining Spanish language skills, creative writing, artistic expression, and teamwork. The story follows three girls on a beach adventure involving a witch, a Cybertruck, a volcano, and Garfield.

Elle Science

Fish and Aquatic Animals - Body Structures and Survival

Kindergarteners are studying goldfish and livebearers: body structures, survival needs, water care. Next up: snails.

Ballard Science

Plants - Germination and Growth

1st graders planted ryegrass, alfalfa, and wheat seeds. Studying germination, growth rates, and plant care. Connects to classroom teacher's current unit.

All PE

Review, Team Sports, and Field Day Prep

K-5 students reviewing year's material: teamwork, sportsmanship, throwing/catching, manipulating objects, cardiovascular endurance. Field Day activities being practiced. Fitnessgram concepts and skills incorporated into warm-ups.

Elle Art

Underwater Animal Paintings and Clay Sculpture

Kindergarteners completing final paintings of underwater animals and beginning a clay sculpture unit. Clay explorations will be sent home.

Ballard Art

Spring Flower Paintings and Clay Sculpture

1st graders completing final paintings depicting spring flowers and beginning a clay sculpture unit. Clay explorations will be sent home.

Edie Other

Chef's Kiss: Cooking & Healthy Habits

May theme focuses on cooking. Students will work with an array of ingredients, practice measurements, and learn about healthy habits. Inspired by world-renowned chef Jean-Georges Vongerichten.

Edie Other

Bilingual Progress Assessment (Spanish & English)

Edie's spring progress report covers all developmental domains in both Spanish and English. Benchmarks are rated: Not Yet, Emerging, Consistently, or Mastered. Greyed-out benchmarks have not yet been targeted by the class.

Edie Science

Parts of Plants We Can Eat (Concrete Jungle theme)

Children learned about edible plant parts in Spanish and English: roots (raíces) like potato and carrot, leaves (hojas) like lettuce and spinach, stems (tallos) like celery and asparagus, seeds (semillas) like corn and beans, and fruits (frutas) like apples, bananas, strawberries, oranges, and blueberries.

Edie Reading
