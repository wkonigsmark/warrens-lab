GAME CONCEPTS: BACKLOG
Phase: Conceptual

Tangram

Drag and rotate geometric shapes to fill a target silhouette outline. Builds spatial reasoning and patience — feels satisfying when pieces snap together cleanly.

Phase: Conceptual

Towers of Hanoi

Move a stack of rings from one peg to another, never placing a larger ring on a smaller one. A pure sequential logic puzzle that scales naturally from 3 to 7 discs.

Phase: Conceptual

Lights Out

Toggle grid tiles to turn all lights off — each press flips the tile and its neighbors. Deceptively simple to understand, genuinely hard to solve. Great for pure logic development.

Phase: Conceptual

Anagram Unscramble

Rearrange scrambled letters to form a valid word. Drag-and-drop interface with tiered difficulty by word length and frequency. Natural complement to the Lexicon vocabulary work.

Phase: Conceptual

Word Weaver (Word Ladder)

A word ladder game inspired by Weaver where you transform a start word into an end word by changing one letter at a time, with each intermediate step being a valid word.
