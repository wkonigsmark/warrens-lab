Fade & Draw

Progressive trace course · Subject: Hand

How it works: Print all 7 pages, do one a day. Each page is built from your own reference drawing — Day 1 gives you the full tonal study to trace, and each day peels away a layer: first the shading, then the fine lines, then the outline itself, until the final days leave only the construction guides fitted to the real hand, and then almost nothing. You trace less and draw more, every day, until the hand lives in your hand.
