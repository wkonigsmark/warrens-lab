Fade & Draw

Progressive trace course · Subject: Hand

How it works: Print all 7 pages. Do one page a day. Each day the template hands you less — first the shading disappears, then the fine detail, then the outline itself, until the last days give you only the underlying construction guides, and finally almost nothing. You trace less and draw more, every single day, until the hand lives in your hand.

🖼️ To add your reference photo: save your finished hand drawing as reference-target.jpg in this folder and it will appear as the "goal" thumbnail on every page. (Optional — the pages work without it.)
