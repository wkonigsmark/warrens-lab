# CHRONOS: The Grand Descent
**Chronos** is a premium, high-performance historical engine built to model the entirety of known time as a continuous "vertical descent." From the **Big Bang** to the present day, Chronos provides a cinematic journey through the milestones of human, scientific, and cosmological history.

---

## 🚀 Key Evolutionary Updates

### 1. Significance & Rubric Engine
The engine now uses a logic-driven **Significance Scoring System (Level 1-3)**.
- **Level 1 (Core Foundations)**: The universal "Operating System" of human history (e.g. Writing, Printing Press, Industrial Revolution).
- **Level 2 (Deep Time & Major Bridges)**: Focused on cosmological formation and prehistoric evolution (e.g. Big Bang, Formation of Earth).
- **Level 3 (Cultural Enrichment)**: High-fidelity details such as specific artistic or musical births (e.g. Beethoven).

### 2. Educational Module: "How Do We Know?"
A dedicated student-facing module exploring the science of dating the past.
- Covers **Carbon-14**, **Stellar Parallax**, **Dendrochronology**, and **Written Records**.
- Dynamic, kid-friendly "Detective Work" overlay for interactive learning.

### 3. Dedicated Resolution Modes
Unlocking Level 2 now enables **Dedicated Viewing Modes**.
- Users can toggle between **Core** and **Dedicated Deep Time** resolutions, allowing focused study of specific historical tiers without clutter.

### 4. Interactive & Worksheet Quiz Suite
The randomization engine is now context-linked to the resolution level selected, allowing for modular testing on specific eras or the entire historical throughline.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

### Active Execution Core
- **`index.html`**: Terminal landing. Integrated with Google Analytics (gtag).
- **`script.js`**: Core UI, Quiz, and Curation logic.
- **`seed_events.js`**: Verified Database (IDs 1-69).
- **`chronos_significance_engine.js`**: Keyword-based scoring for automated significance suggestion.
- **`edu_module.js`**: Educational content data layer.
- **`style.css`**: Advanced UI styles, glassmorphism, and cinematic descent animations.
