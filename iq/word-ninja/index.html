Speed Sight Word Challenge

Word Ninja is a reading fluency tool that builds speed and confidence with sight words and classic literature.

Two Modes:

  • Sight Words: Random drill words from your grade level to build vocabulary speed
  • Literature: Sequential passages from classic public domain books (available for K, 1st, 5th+, and Adult)

How to Play:

  1. Select a Time Limit (15, 30, 45, or 60 seconds)
  2. Choose your Grade Level
  3. Pick a Mode (if available for your grade)
  4. Click Start Game
  5. Click NEXT ✓ for correct words or SKIP ✗ for words to review
  6. Beat your best score!

Tip: Best scores are tracked separately for each mode, grade, and time limit. Want to preview passages? Click "View All Passages" below!
