Model Definitions

These are deliberately simple, transparent metrics so we can argue with them productively.

Total Strength

Sum the four W-Index scores in the group. Higher means more total quality.

Parity

Sum the absolute W-Index difference across all six group matchups. Lower means more even.

Close Density

Count each unique group matchup once, out of six total games, within 5, 10, and 15 W-Index points.
