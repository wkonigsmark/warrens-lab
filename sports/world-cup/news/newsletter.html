Notable Deviations

Our model slightly overestimated goal totals early on, likely due to weather conditions we didn't fully weight in our feature engineering. However, the underdog win rate tracked closely, suggesting our team strength ratings were well-calibrated.

The biggest surprise? Scotland's defensive resilience. We had them generating 0.8 expected defensive actions per 90, but they actually hit 1.2. This is the kind of variance that keeps pool players on their toes!
