7 Year Old Baseball • Master Practice Plan

The Rockies 60-Minute Master Plan

Phase 1: Consecutive Catch

0:00 - 0:07

Concept: Pair players by skill. Start 15ft apart. Goal: 10 consecutive toss-and-catches. Milestone reached? 2 steps back. This builds early hand-eye rhythm and arm heat before we huddle.

Phase 2: Diamond Dogs

0:07 - 0:15

Full team sprints around the bases. Boys MUST cheer their teammates to earn their turn.

Coach Note: Round the bases! If they miss a bag, the time isn't recorded. TOUCH EVERY BASE.

Phase 3: Stretch, Warmup & Focus

0:15 - 0:23

5m Stretch: Transition from energy to FOCUS with a **Stillness Challenge**: See how long they can close their eyes and remain silent/still.

Key Rule Huddle (3m):
  • Rules: 5-run limit, all bat, 10 in the field.
  • Philosophy: Take risks! No penalties for missed throws to 1st or home.

Phase 4: "Field Commander" Scramble

0:23 - 0:31

Positional awareness drill. Call out positions and have them sprint to the bag as quickly as possible.

Phase 5: Dual Cohort Rotations (16m Total • 0:31 - 0:47)

Cohort A: Force Out Logic

Infield Focus

Fielding at SS and 2B. Focus on bag coverage at 2nd and accurate throws to 1st. 8m blocks per cohort.

Cohort B: Cutoff Man

Outfield Focus

Outfielder runs down ball, defers to first teammate, and fires a hard, quick release toward 2B. No running it in!

Phase 6: Live Sim & On-Deck Lab

0:47 - 0:57

Live Sim: 5 swings each. Every contacted ball MUST be fielded and thrown to 1st base (or 2B for cutoff simulation).

Station B (Integrated): 1 coach works with the On-Deck Batter. Focusing on the "Power & Hip-Turn" mechanics. Batter gets several high-quality practice swings before coming to the plate.

Phase 7: The Summit

0:57 - 1:00

Scale the Mountain: Reinforce the incredible progress made today. Remind them to keep breaking in their gloves at home!

True Rockies Spirit: Emphasize being a selfless teammate. Be ready to sit the bench, play the outfield, or bat low in the order—stay focused, stay supportive, and cheer for your brothers.

1 - 2 - 3 ROCKIES!
