How To Play

A tangram is a 7-piece puzzle: 5 triangles, 1 square, and 1 parallelogram. Arrange them so they exactly fill the dashed shape.

Controls

  • Tap a piece to select it.
  • Use the ↺ and ↻ buttons above the selected piece to rotate by 45°.
  • The parallelogram also shows a ⇆ flip button — it's the only piece that ever needs flipping.
  • Drag pieces freely; they don't snap.
