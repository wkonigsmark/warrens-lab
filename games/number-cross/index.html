How It Works

Cross out the extras

Every row and column has a target sum shown outside the grid. Cross out numbers inside so the remaining values add up to the target on every row and every column.

Click a cell once to mark it as a keeper (circled). Click again to cross it out. A third click clears it back to unmarked.

The live "current / target" readout next to each total tells you where you stand. When every row and column glows green, you've solved it.

Pick Easy for small numbers, Hard for full 1–9 sums. Hit Print to take a puzzle off-screen.
